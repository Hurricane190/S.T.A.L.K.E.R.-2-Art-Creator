Third-party tools bundled with S2 Art Creator
=============================================

S2 Art Creator itself is a separate application. The following command-line
tools are shipped next to it and are invoked as separate processes.

1) retoc, repak, texconv, oo2core_9_win64.dll
   Unreal Engine / IoStore and texture tooling used by the existing artwork,
   menu and music pipelines. See the upstream projects for their licences.

2) wav2wem.exe  (GPL-2.0)
   Converts WAV/FLAC to Wwise Vorbis .wem files without Wwise.
   Project / source: https://github.com/pas2k/wav2wem
   Licence:         see wav2wem-LICENSE.txt (GNU GPL v2)
   Third-party:     see wav2wem-THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md
   The binary embeds oggenc2 (a GPLv2 build of vorbis-tools) and the aoTuV
   b6.03 codebooks. The complete corresponding source is available at the
   project URL above; the binary is redistributed unmodified.

3) vgmstream-cli.exe and its DLLs  (ISC licence)
   Audio preview decoder used by the Music tab.
   Project / source: https://github.com/vgmstream/vgmstream
   Licence:         see COPYING
   Redistributed unmodified, together with the runtime DLLs from the official
   Windows build.

These tools are used only to read audio from your own game installation and to
encode your own audio files. No game audio is redistributed with the app.
